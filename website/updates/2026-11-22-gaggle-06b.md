Title: Gaggle 0.6B: pretraining and reranking
Summary: A 0.55B-parameter base model and its supervised pointwise reranker.
Model: 0.6b

Gaggle 0.6B is the smallest member of Project Greenhouse’s Gaggle family. The 0.55B-parameter decoder was trained from scratch with [nanochat](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat) on 287B tokens of [ClimbMix](https://huggingface.co/datasets/karpathy/climbmix-400b-shuffle). The team chose a 28-layer, 1,024-wide architecture after comparing four shapes at a fixed 7B-token budget. This shape had the lowest validation loss and highest CORE in that sweep.

## From base model to reranker

The base checkpoint is a text completion model. Starting from those weights, we trained a pointwise relevance scorer for one epoch on [RLHN-250K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rlhn/rlhn-250K) using localized contrastive estimation. The released reranker has 0.52B parameters because its scoring head keeps only the two output rows used to score relevance.

## Evaluation

When reranking BM25’s top 100 candidates, Gaggle 0.6B scored **0.634** mean nDCG@10 across TREC DL 2019–2023 and **0.533** across seven BEIR collections. The corresponding 0.9B scores were 0.632 and 0.533. The linked model cards document the per-collection scores and training settings.
