Title: Gaggle 0.6B: pretraining and reranking
Summary: A 0.55B-parameter base model and its supervised pointwise reranker.
Model: 0.6b

Gaggle 0.6B is the smallest member of Project Greenhouse’s Gaggle family. The 0.55B-parameter decoder was trained from scratch with [nanochat](https://github.com/karpathy/nanochat) on 287B tokens of [ClimbMix](https://huggingface.co/datasets/karpathy/climbmix-400b-shuffle).

## Architecture sweep

Each candidate saw 7B training tokens. Validation loss is measured in bits per byte (lower is better). CORE was measured at the final step using 500 examples per task.

| Layers × width | Value embeddings every | Parameters | Final validation bits/byte | Final CORE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 × 1,024 (stock nanochat) | 2 layers | 536.9M | 0.7562 | 0.2272 |
| 32 × 896 | 4 layers | 601.9M | 0.7403 | 0.2403 |
| 24 × 1,024 | 4 layers | 570.4M | 0.7413 | 0.2389 |
| **28 × 1,024 (selected)** | **7 layers** | **553.6M** | **0.7373** | **0.2417** |

We selected the 28-layer shape for its lowest validation loss. Its CORE score was close to the other two deeper shapes. It used about 65% more FLOPs per token than stock nanochat at a similar parameter count.

## From base model to reranker

The base checkpoint is a text completion model. Starting from those weights, we trained a pointwise relevance scorer for one epoch on [RLHN-250K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rlhn/rlhn-250K) using localized contrastive estimation. The released reranker has 0.52B parameters because its scoring head keeps only the two output rows used to score relevance.
